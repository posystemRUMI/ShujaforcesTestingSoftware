A supersonic fighter aircraft executes a high-G pull-up maneuver with radius R = 800 meters at a steady velocity of 400 m/s. Calculate the total centripetal acceleration experienced by the pilot airframe in multiples of gravity (g = 9.8 m/s²).

A 10.2 G
B 20.4 G (Approx. ~20.41 G)
C 5.1 G
D 31.8 G
verified Faculty Solution Walkthrough

Formula: a_c = v² / R. a_c = (400 m/s)² / (800 m) = 160,000 / 800 = 200 m/s². In G-load: a_c / g = 200 / 9.8 = 20.408 G ≈ 20.4 G.
